Welcome to DoBu Martial Arts

Founded by Mauricio Gomez (4th Dan Judo, 3rd Dan Jiu-Jitsu), DoBu Martial Arts is a premier gym dedicated to teaching traditional and modern martial arts in a welcoming environment.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced martial artist, our expert coaches will help you achieve your goals — from fitness and self-defence to competition.

  • Large matted martial arts area
  • Fully-equipped gym & fitness room
  • Sauna & steam room
  • Changing & shower facilities
