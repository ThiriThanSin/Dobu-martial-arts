Our Story

DoBu Martial Arts was founded by Mauricio Gomez — a 4th Dan Blackbelt in Judo, 3rd Dan in Jiu-Jitsu, 1st Dan in Karate, and an accredited Muay Thai coach. After decades of training and competition, Mauricio set out to create a gym where traditional martial arts values meet modern training methods.

Today, DoBu is home to a thriving community of martial artists — from complete beginners to experienced competitors. Our philosophy is simple: respect, discipline, and continuous improvement.
